THE DATA WE USE

Historical windows, with a clear chronology.

2014–2024 / MICHIGAN

The original research dataset contains 363,419 county/time windows. Each target indicates whether the next three complete hourly county means reach the county's training-period 90th-percentile outage-burden threshold. A missing reading is never assumed to mean zero outages.

03
TRAIN & SELECT A THRESHOLD

Train the boosted-tree model.

READY

Train on the 20,000-row historical subset through 180 boosting iterations. Then choose a threshold on the 4,000-row 2023 validation subset: maximize recall with false-positive rate ≤2% and at least 25 alerts. No 2024 test predictions are made in this step.

    The first run downloads Python and scientific packages. Training runs in a background browser worker. The published compressed subset is about 3.3 MB; its checksum is verified before fitting.
